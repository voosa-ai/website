01 / How it works

Build. Vote. Architect.

You don't build for Voosa. Build however you like, then bring it home. Three steps, zero gatekeeping.

01

Build anywhere

Anyone on your team can build apps, in any tool. Voosa hosts them all, versions them, and puts every one behind your company login.

02

Vote

Every build lands in your company's app catalog. People try each other's tools and upvote what actually helps. The best ideas rise on merit, not job title.

03

Architect

Voosa connects the winners to shared company records (clients, sites, projects, people) and spots overlaps, so a pile of separate tools becomes one system.

02 / Compliance

Compliant by default.

Your builders shouldn't have to think about audit trails. On Voosa, every app inherits them automatically, so a technician's weekend build can carry the same compliance weight as enterprise software.

03 / Ownership

Your special sauce stays yours.

The tools your team builds are your competitive edge. Voosa never owns them. We provide the plumbing; you keep every line of code.

  • You own your code. Every app you bring stays your intellectual property.
  • No lock-in. Export any app and its data whenever you want.
  • Keep your tools. Build in whatever AI tool your team prefers, today and tomorrow.

Your work is the keystone.

In an arch, the keystone is the piece everything depends on. In your company, it's what your people build: the tools that capture how you do the work better than anyone else. Voosa is the stones on either side, holding your keystone in place. We support the tools that make you unique.
