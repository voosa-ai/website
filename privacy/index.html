Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Voosa, LLC ("Voosa", "we", "us") makes software that hosts the apps a company's people build. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website, voosa.ai, and the Voosa apps you sign in to.

Information we collect

When you sign in. You sign in to Voosa apps through Auth0, our sign-in provider. You can use a Google account, a Microsoft work account, an email code or a password. We receive:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Your company's Google Workspace domain or Microsoft tenant ID, if you sign in with a work account.
  • A user ID, and the dates and times you sign in.

We ask Google and Microsoft only for your basic profile and email address. We do not read your email, files, calendar or contacts.

When you use a Voosa app. We store the records you and your colleagues enter, such as forms, reports and sign-offs. We also keep an audit trail of who changed what, and when. Your company controls this content.

When you contact us. If you email us or ask for early access, we keep your email address and what you send us.

When you visit this website. This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run the apps and show you the records you have access to.
  • To keep audit trails that your company may need for compliance.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To fix problems and improve the service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not use your company's content to train AI models.

Google user data

Voosa's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use the name, email address and profile picture Google provides only to sign you in and show who you are inside Voosa apps. We do not transfer this data to others except the service providers listed below, and only to run Voosa.

Who we share information with

We share personal information only with companies that help us run Voosa, and only for that purpose:

  • Auth0 (Okta) for sign-in.
  • Amazon Web Services for hosting and data storage in the United States.
  • Google and Microsoft, when you choose to sign in with them.

Your company can see the content and audit trail in its own Voosa apps. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or our service.

How long we keep information

We keep account information while your account is active. We keep app records and audit trails for as long as your company's agreement with us requires, which may follow your company's records-retention rules. When an account or agreement ends, we delete or return the data within a reasonable time, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest. We limit access to the people who need it to run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected people about a breach as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete your personal information. If your company provides your Voosa account, we may pass your request to your company, because it controls its content. You can also remove Voosa's access in your Google account settings at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights under local law.

Children

Voosa is a business service. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell account holders by email or in the app before it takes effect.
